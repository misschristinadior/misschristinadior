History

A factual year-by-year record of Christina Dior’s career. Open a year to read it; the Performance Archive contains the deeper poster and repertoire collection.

2001Beginnings in Sydney

Beginnings in Sydney. Christina Dior began performing in Sydney's drag scene, developing a repertoire combining showgirl glamour, diva impersonations, dance-pop and theatrical comedy. Early appearances included Polly's Follies and the Venus Room. Christina also began developing the custom music-editing techniques that became a feature of her performances.

2008Performance & video production

Sydney performances and video production. Christina's work included ARQ Sydney, community events and productions associated with Polly Petrie and Kirsten Damned. She also developed original video productions, including DragTube and edited Dragstar Search highlights prepared for screening during club nights. Her performance work extended to touring engagements, including a drag entertainment cruise during this period.

2009Recurring Sydney productions

Recurring Sydney productions. Christina became a regular presence at the Midnight Shift through Pretty Women, a recurring Friday-night drag show documented in contemporary Sydney press listings. Her work at Taxi Club included themed productions, comedy and musical numbers, and collaborations with other Sydney entertainers.

ReferencesStar Observer — Scene GuideStar Observer — What's On
2010DIVA Rising Star

DIVA Rising Star. Christina received the DIVA Rising Star Award, recognising her work within Sydney's drag entertainment scene. She continued hosting and performing at Stonewall Hotel, ARQ, Taxi Club, Midnight Shift and Sly Fox, including substitute appearances for other established performers. Notable engagements included a Madonna tribute at Sydney's final Sleaze Ball, The Reunion at the Burdekin Hotel, ARQ Christmas performances and Stonewall's New Year's Eve Masquerade Ball. Her work also included fundraising performances for community organisations.

2011Hosting, TV & national appearances

Hosting, television and national appearances. Christina appeared in numerous Sydney productions and community events, including Prisoner: Cell Block Heaven, Mardi Gras celebrations, ARQ's White Party marriage-equality fundraiser and Heaven Social Dance's anniversary fundraiser. She hosted Stonewall's Mardi Gras parade celebrations and continued regular hosting and performance work at Stonewall, ARQ and Taxi Club. Further credits included an Australian television commercial promoting Dolly Parton's Better Day tour, a Dolly Parton-themed Stonewall production and an appearance at Darwin Pride's Queens of the Galaxy. Her charitable work included performances benefiting Queensland flood victims and LGBTQ community organisations.

ReferencesStar Observer — Stonewall's Big Porn PartyStar Observer — Stonewall hostingBeverly Buttercup historical archive
2012DIVA Miss Congeniality & press

DIVA Miss Congeniality and major press coverage. Christina and Minnie Cooper jointly received the DIVA Miss Congeniality Award. Christina was featured on the cover of City Hub in an article about the Taxi Club's reprieve from closure and performed in its Easter celebrations alongside Ophelia Shaft. Her other work included Monday Night Madness at Stonewall Hotel, where she appeared as a Cher impersonator, hosting Drag Bingo at Crows Nest Hotel, and performing at Parramatta's Rainbow Party. She also contributed guest appearances and community updates to Queer Out West radio.

2014International media coverage

International media coverage. A filmed performance of Whitney Houston's Saving All My Love for You at Sydney's Stonewall Hotel was selected for inclusion in Philadelphia Magazine's G Philly feature celebrating seven Whitney Houston drag performances. The American publication featured Christina alongside other drag artists, bringing her Australian performance footage to an international online audience.

DIVA anniversary media appearance. Christina participated in the 25th anniversary DIVA Awards SX magazine cover shoot at Stonewall Hotel, alongside past award winners and other established Sydney performers.

2016Polly's Follies retrospective

Recognition in Sydney drag history. A historical retrospective celebrating 25 years of Polly's Follies identified Christina Dior among the performers who began their careers through the production, documenting her place in Sydney's earlier drag entertainment scene.

2017Prada's Priscillas touring

Prada's Priscillas touring production. Christina joined Prada Clutch's touring theatrical drag revue as one of the supporting showgirls alongside Conchita Grande, with performances extending beyond Sydney to regional Australian theatres.

ReferencesNewcastle Herald — A fab clutch of queens
2018Prada's Priscillas review

Prada's Priscillas touring production. Christina performed as one of Prada Clutch's supporting showgirls alongside Conchita Grande in Prada's Priscillas, a touring theatrical drag revue presented in regional Australian theatres. Appearances included Newcastle's Civic Theatre and Canberra Theatre. The Canberra production received a published review from the Canberra Critics Circle, which identified Christina by name and discussed the production's professionalism and elaborate staging.

ReferencesCanberra Critics Circle — Prada's Priscillas
2019Relocation to Europe

Relocation to Europe. Christina relocated from Australia to France, following nearly two decades of involvement in Sydney drag entertainment, hosting, theatre, video production and costume design.

2022A new chapter in Berlin

A new chapter in Berlin. Christina relocated to Berlin, Germany, beginning a new period of artistic activity and involvement in the city's queer nightlife and drag performance community.

2023SchwuZ Drag Open Stage

SchwuZ Drag Open Stage. Christina joined SchwuZ's Drag Open Stage Class and appeared in the club's performance programme. Her appearances included the Pepsi Boston Bar Drag Open Stage and SchwuZ's 46th anniversary celebrations. German LGBTQ publication men named Christina among the performers appearing at the anniversary event.

Referencesmen* — 46 years SchwuZVictory Column October program
2024Berlin & original music

Berlin performances and original music. Christina continued performing at SchwuZ, including the Pepsi Boston Bar's Lovegame and themed Drag Open Stage productions. She also produced promotional artwork for the venue. During the year, Christina began releasing self-written, AI-assisted original music under the stylised recording name Christina Dior. Releases included Steal the Night, Time for Me, Tonight Tonight Tonight, Slay All Night and Midnight Glow, alongside multiple remixes and alternative arrangements.

ReferencesVictory Column — Love GameChristina Dïor on Apple Music
2025Original-music catalogue

Expansion of the original-music catalogue. Christina continued writing and producing original recordings, releasing Just Stream That Track, One Day Lover, Rail Me on the U-Bahn, BBC, May Day May Day and In the Darkroom. Her work incorporated dance music, electronic arrangements, vocal production, humour and themes drawn from nightlife and performance culture. Christina also continued developing accompanying artwork, remixes and video projects.

ReferencesChristina Dïor — Apple Music
2026Digital stage production & archive

Digital stage production and archive preservation. Christina expanded her online creative work with a collection of original animated stage backgrounds designed for drag shows, concerts, karaoke and other performances. The videos attracted tens of thousands of views on YouTube. Alongside continued music and video development, she began assembling a comprehensive digital archive documenting 25 years of performances, productions, costumes, media appearances and original recordings.
