History

A factual record of Christina Dior’s career. Select a year to open it; detailed show dates, posters and repertoire remain in the Performance Archive.

2001Beginnings in Sydney

Christina Dior began performing in Sydney, developing a style rooted in showgirl glamour, dance-pop performance and queer nightlife.

Early work was connected with Polly’s Follies and the Venus Room in Kings Cross, where Christina also developed music-editing and drag-DJ skills.

2006Palace Hotel

A surviving performance photograph documents a Kylie-themed show at the Palace Hotel.

2008ARQ, Fair Day and Sydney circuit

Christina’s Sydney circuit included ARQ, Taxi Club and community appearances. Shimtastic appeared on the Fair Day main stage, while other work included performance projects with Polly Petrie and Kirsten Damned.

2009Taxi Club and Pretty Women

The Taxi Club became a major home for themed shows and recurring productions. At the Midnight Shift, Pretty Women became a recurring Christina Dior show.

2010DIVA Rising Star

Christina received the DIVA Rising Star award. Regular performance work continued across Taxi Club, Stonewall Hotel, Midnight Shift, ARQ and Sly Fox, alongside frequent fill-in appearances for Maxi Shield, Verushka Darling and Tora Hymen.

The year also included the final Sleaze Ball, The Reunion, and XXXmas Eve at ARQ.

2011National appearances and major Sydney work

A particularly active year included the Queensland Floods Drag-A-Thon, Fair Day and Mardi Gras-season work, Prisoner: Cell Block Heaven, ARQ’s White Party, and extensive Stonewall, Taxi Club and ARQ appearances.

Christina appeared in an Australian television commercial promoting Dolly Parton’s Better Day tour alongside Maxi Shield and Laydee Kinme, and travelled to Darwin for the inaugural Queens of the Galaxy event during Darwin Pride.

2012Miss Congeniality and Taxi Club press

Christina and Minnie Cooper were joint recipients of the DIVA Miss Congeniality award.

City Hub published “Hail the Taxi – Christina Dior celebrates the Taxi Club’s reprieve.” Christina also appeared at the Rainbow Party in Parramatta and made several guest appearances and city-update call-ins on Queer Out West 89.3 FM.

2013The Delta Riggs — Rah Rah Radio

Christina appeared in The Delta Riggs’ official music video for Rah Rah Radio, credited as “Miss Christina Dior”.

2014International online feature

Philadelphia Magazine / G Philly featured Christina’s performance of Whitney Houston’s Saving All My Love for You in a Whitney Houston drag-performance feature. The performance itself was filmed at Stonewall Hotel in Sydney.

2018Prada’s Priscillas

Christina toured as a featured supporting showgirl in Prada’s Priscillas, appearing with Prada Clutch and Conchita Grande at regional theatres including Newcastle and Canberra.

2019Move to Europe

Christina relocated from Australia to Europe after a long career spanning queer venues, private and corporate events, television, theatre and touring work.

2022Berlin

Christina moved to Berlin, beginning a new chapter in the city’s queer nightlife and performance scene.

2023SchwuZ

Christina appeared at SchwuZ, including Drag Open Stage programming and the club’s 46th anniversary celebrations.

2024Original music begins

Berlin performances continued while Christina began releasing original music under the Christina Dïor name, including Steal the Night, Luv Urself, Time for Me, More Makeup, Tonight Tonight Tonight, It’s My Birthday, Dancestorm, Slay All Night and Midnight Glow.

2025–2026Music, video and archive work

The original-music catalogue expanded with releases including One Day Lover, Leaving You on Read, In the Darkroom and Rail Me on the U-Bahn. Current work brings together music, performance, video production and preservation of Christina’s 25-year archive.
