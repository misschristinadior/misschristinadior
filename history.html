History

A factual year-by-year record of Christina Dior’s career. Open a year to read it; the Performance Archive contains the deeper poster and repertoire collection.

2001Sydney performances & early career

Beginnings in Sydney. Christina Dior began performing in Sydney’s drag scene, developing a repertoire combining showgirl glamour, diva impersonations, dance-pop and theatrical comedy. After The Albury Hotel closed in 2001, Polly’s Follies moved to the Venus Room in Kings Cross; Christina’s first Polly’s Follies appearance was during the Venus Room period.

Young Talent Mime at ARQ Sydney. Christina also performed in Young Talent Mime, an early Thursday-night showcase at ARQ that gave emerging drag performers stage experience. The programme later evolved into Drag for Dollars, after Christina’s involvement had ended. She also began developing the custom music-editing techniques that became a feature of her performances.

ReferencesDrag Queens of 2000–2010 retrospective
2002Sydney performances & venues

Sydney club performances. Christina continued performing across Sydney’s drag circuit, including guest spots at Polly’s Follies during the Venus Room period. She also performed at the Flinders Hotel alongside Rusty Boxxx and Sandy Bottom.

2003Sydney performances & club circuit

Sydney performances. Christina continued across Sydney’s drag circuit and remained connected with Polly’s Follies as the production moved through its post-Albury venue era. Her club repertoire and custom-edited production tracks continued to develop during this period.

2004Sydney performances, promotion & media

Sydney performance work. Christina continued performing across the Sydney circuit, including recurring work connected with Polly’s Follies.

Connie and Carla promotion. Christina participated in Sydney promotional activities for the film Connie and Carla, alongside Polly Petrie and Kirsten Damned. Surviving photographs document the three performers travelling together during the promotion.

ARQ retrospective filming. Christina filmed retrospective material about the early Young Talent Mime era and ARQ Sydney. Material from this period was later preserved in her video archive and the Drag Queens of 2000–2010 retrospective.

ReferencesDrag Queens of 2000–2010 retrospective
2005Sydney performances & Polly’s Follies

Sydney club work. Christina continued guest appearances with Polly’s Follies and other Sydney performances. During this mid-2000s period, Polly’s Follies was running its Sunday show at the Lizard Lounge within the Exchange Hotel in Darlinghurst after its earlier Venus Room period.

Press archive. Christina’s surviving archive also includes 2005 SX magazine material connected with Polly’s Follies.

2006Sydney performances & club circuit

Polly’s Follies — Lizard Lounge / Exchange Hotel. Christina continued performing in the long-running Sunday show at the Lizard Lounge within the Exchange Hotel, Darlinghurst. The production remained there for several years before its later move to Stonewall Hotel.

Palace Hotel. Christina’s work also included a Kylie Minogue-themed performance at the Palace Hotel with Vanity, owner of Wigs by Vanity, and the late Maxi Shield. The show is preserved in photographs taken by Peteez/Imagez Photography.

2007Performance, design, video & NaviGAYtion

Sydney club work. Christina continued guest appearances with Polly’s Follies and performed shows at the Taxi Club.

NaviGAYtion. Christina performed aboard NaviGAYtion, the Sydney-based all-gay cruise aboard P&O’s Pacific Sun, sailing from 29 March to 1 April 2007. Her archive includes photographs from the onboard drag entertainment.

Fashion design and music video appearances. Christina designed and constructed a floor-length pink gown from Supré shopping bags for the closing look of Supré’s New Zealand Fashion Week presentation in Auckland. She also appeared alongside Kirsten Damned in the music video for Viva Las Vegas by Australian Elvis tribute performer She Is the King (Jacqueline Feilich).

2008Performance, theatre & video production

Polly’s Follies and Sydney performances. By 2008, Christina’s recurring Polly’s Follies work included the show’s Stonewall Hotel era alongside her other club appearances.

Carlotta’s Priscilla Show. Christina Dior won the grand final of Carlotta’s Priscilla Show competition at Stonewall Hotel, receiving a $1,000 cash prize and a position in Carlotta’s Priscilla Show. Although the original prize included a one-year position, Christina continued performing in the production until it stopped running. She also designed and constructed costumes for the show.

Dragged to the Casino / Dragged to the Star. During this period Christina was part of a recurring Good Friday drag production staged around Sydney’s casino entertainment precinct, taking advantage of the unusually quiet Good Friday nightlife calendar.

DragTube and original video production. Christina developed and presented DragTube, documenting Sydney and regional LGBTQ nightlife through venue reports, street interviews, performances, backstage footage, travel and conversations with fellow entertainers. A separate Drag Queens of 2000–2010 retrospective preserved interviews, performances and recollections from Sydney’s drag community.

ReferencesStar Observer — Star Play, 20 April 2008DragTube 001Drag Queens of 2000–2010
2009Residencies, community events & performances

Pretty Woman — Midnight Shift. Pretty Woman began in late 2009 at the Midnight Shift, becoming a recurring Friday-night production. During the run Christina also had sponsorship support from Soleil Tanning.

Stonewall, Polly’s Follies and regional work. Christina continued Sydney club work including Polly’s Follies at Stonewall Hotel, and performed at events including Pure Nightclub’s Afterglow and Pure Pride / Double Dose.

Community and fundraising. Christina participated in Shop Yourself Stupid and in ARQ/ACON’s Under the Mirrorball; surviving archive material for the 2009 event records more than $10,000 raised for ACON.

ReferencesStar Observer — Scene GuideStar Observer — What’s On
2010Performance, television, awards & fundraising

Sydney performance circuit. Christina continued Stonewall Hotel late-night appearances, Polly’s Follies guest spots and regular fill-in work for Maxi Shield and Verushka Darling at the Midnight Shift and Columbian Hotel. She also performed with Tora Hymen at Drag Bingo at the Kings Cross Hotel.

DIVA Rising Star. Christina received the 2010 DIVA Rising Star Award. Other engagements included Sly Fox, a Madonna tribute at Sydney’s final Sleaze Ball, The Reunion at the Burdekin Hotel, ARQ Christmas performances and Stonewall New Year’s Eve celebrations.

Design and special events. Christina made a dress for Maxi Shield for a Lady Gaga performance and appeared on the Salute to Summer cruise with Tora Hymen and Natasha Knowles.

Fundraising. Christina again participated in Under the Mirrorball; surviving 2010 archive material records more than $8,000 raised for ACON.

Television — Underbelly: The Golden Mile. Christina appeared in the third instalment of the Australian crime drama Underbelly. Production began in Sydney in 2009 and the series was broadcast in 2010.

2011Performance, television, business & community

Sydney performances and hosting. Christina continued Stonewall Hotel late-night appearances, Polly’s Follies and Drag Bingo with Tora Hymen at the Kings Cross Hotel. Her wider 2011 work included Prisoner: Cell Block Heaven, Mardi Gras celebrations, ARQ’s White Night marriage-equality fundraiser, Heaven Social Dance and Stonewall Mardi Gras hosting.

ARQ and themed productions. Christina performed at ARQ Christmas events and appeared in a Kylie Minogue tribute night with Minnie Cooper. Sydney Star Observer coverage also documented the wider DIVA Awards scene during the year.

Shimtastic Shop. Christina registered Shimtastic Shop in 2011. The shop sold makeup, costumes and performance-related items; surviving photographs document the storefront, interior and branded merchandise.

Television and national appearances. Credits included an Australian television commercial promoting Dolly Parton’s Better Day tour with Laydee Kinme, a Dolly Parton-themed Stonewall production, Darwin Pride’s Queens of the Galaxy, and the ABC legal drama Crownies.

Community fundraising. Christina participated in the Queensland Floods Drag-A-Thon, which surviving material records as raising $3,150, and in Under the Mirrorball, with archive material recording more than $8,000 raised for ACON. She also co-hosted the Heaven Social Dance anniversary fundraiser benefiting ACON West.

2012Awards, hosting, press & community

DIVA Miss Congeniality and major press coverage. Christina and Minnie Cooper jointly received the 2012 DIVA Miss Congeniality Award. Christina was also featured on the cover of City Hub in an article about the Taxi Club’s reprieve from closure and performed in its Easter celebrations alongside Ophelia Shaft.

Stonewall, Kinselas and hosting. Christina hosted and DJed Monday Night Madness at Stonewall Hotel, playing international hits and performing character and impersonation material including Cher and Lady Gaga. She also occasionally hosted Stonewall karaoke and guest-starred on some Friday and Saturday nights at Kinselas. The Stonewall production SMASH also ran in 2012.

Drag Bingo and community appearances. Christina hosted Drag Bingo at Crows Nest Hotel, appeared at Parramatta’s Rainbow Party, contributed guest appearances and community updates to Queer Out West radio, and continued involvement with Shop Yourself Stupid.

Fundraising and ARQ. Her 2012 archive includes ARQ’s Countdown to Christmas and the tenth annual Under the Mirrorball World AIDS Day fundraiser for ACON.

2013Performance, media & hosting

Sydney performances and hosting. Christina continued Stonewall Hotel late-night performances, Polly’s Follies guest appearances and occasional karaoke hosting. The Stonewall production Les salopes de la mode was part of the 2013 performance period.

The Delta Riggs music video. Christina appeared in the official music video for Rah Rah Radio by Australian rock band The Delta Riggs, credited as Miss Christina Dior.

Polly Petrie’s birthday at Slide. Christina performed at Slide Sydney for Polly Petrie’s birthday in 2013; surviving video footage documents the performance.

2014Performance, television & media

Sydney performances and hosting. Christina continued Stonewall Hotel late-night performances, Polly’s Follies guest appearances and occasional karaoke hosting.

International media coverage. A filmed performance of Whitney Houston’s Saving All My Love for You at Stonewall Hotel was selected for inclusion in Philadelphia Magazine’s G Philly feature celebrating Whitney Houston drag performances.

Television production — Carlotta. Christina also worked on the Australian biographical telemovie Carlotta, broadcast on ABC1 in 2014. This television credit was separate from Christina’s earlier live work in Carlotta’s Priscilla Show.

Sydney performances. Christina continued Polly’s Follies guest appearances, Stonewall late-night shows and occasional karaoke hosting.

DIVA anniversary media appearance. Christina participated in the 25th-anniversary DIVA Awards SX magazine cover shoot at Stonewall Hotel, alongside past award winners and other established Sydney performers.

2016Sydney hosting, Polly’s history & Bathurst

Stonewall hosting. Christina hosted Friday and Saturday nights at Stonewall Hotel while filling in for Marilyn Mootrub during this period.

Polly’s Follies retrospective. A historical retrospective celebrating 25 years of Polly’s Follies identified Christina Dior among performers who began their careers through the production.

Bathurst. Christina also travelled to Bathurst for regional shows at the Oxford Hotel, including a documented appearance on 9 December 2016.

References25 Years of Polly’s Follies
2017Touring, Stonewall, Bathurst & fundraising

Sydney and regional performances. Christina continued Stonewall Hotel late-night appearances and returned to the Oxford Hotel in Bathurst, with documented 2017 dates including 13 January and 31 March, among several Bathurst appearances across the period.

Prada’s Priscillas. Christina joined Prada Clutch’s touring theatrical drag revue as a supporting showgirl alongside Conchita Grande, with performances extending beyond Sydney to regional Australian theatres.

Stonewall Red Party. Christina participated in the 2017 Stonewall Red Party fundraiser for ACON; her archive records more than $4,000 raised.

ReferencesNewcastle Herald — Prada’s Priscillas
2018Touring, Sydney performances & press

Sydney performances. Christina continued Stonewall Hotel late-night appearances and Polly’s Follies guest spots.

Prada’s Priscillas touring production. Christina performed as one of Prada Clutch’s supporting showgirls alongside Conchita Grande in Prada’s Priscillas, including Newcastle’s Civic Theatre and Canberra Theatre. The Canberra production received a published review identifying Christina by name, and the touring show was also covered in a 2018 Focus feature in the Great Lakes/Manning region.

ReferencesCanberra Critics Circle — Prada’s PriscillasNewcastle Herald
2019Relocation to Europe

Relocation to Europe. Christina relocated from Australia to France, following nearly two decades of involvement in Sydney drag entertainment, hosting, theatre, video production and costume design.

2020–2021COVID-19 hiatus

COVID-19 hiatus. Christina’s public performance activity paused during the COVID-19 pandemic, creating a break between the Australian/France era and the later return to performance and creative work in Berlin.

2022Berlin return & CINAEDVS

A new chapter in Berlin. After the COVID-19 break, Christina relocated to Berlin and returned to creative activity within the city’s queer nightlife and drag performance community. During this period, her creative brand Shimtastic was rebranded as CINAEDVS.

2023Berlin performances

SchwuZ Drag Open Stage. Christina joined SchwuZ’s Drag Open Stage Class and appeared in the club’s performance programme, including the Pepsi Boston Bar Drag Open Stage and SchwuZ’s 46th-anniversary celebrations.

Berlin performances and original music. Christina continued performing at SchwuZ, including Pepsi Boston Bar’s Lovegame and themed Drag Open Stage productions, and produced promotional artwork for the venue. She also began releasing self-written original music, with tracks produced and compiled in digital audio workstations for final arrangements and mixes.

2025Music, CINAEDVS web tools & Forever Bound

Music and digital production. Christina continued writing, producing and compiling tracks in DAWs, releasing One Day Lover, Rail Me on the U-Bahn, BBC, May Day May Day and In the Darkroom, alongside artwork, remixes and video projects.

CINAEDVS web tools. The CINAEDVS brand expanded into web productivity tools, including Organon.

Forever Bound. The online TV show Forever Bound began in 2025 as a CINAEDVS creative project.

ReferencesChristina Dïor — Apple MusicCINAEDVSOrganonForever Bound
2026Digital production, games, archive & Backroom

Digital stage production, archive preservation and nightlife media. Christina expanded her online creative work with original animated stage backgrounds for drag shows, concerts and karaoke, continued music and video development, and began assembling a comprehensive digital archive documenting 25 years of performances, productions, costumes, media appearances and recordings. She also became the leading creative producer behind Backroom, a gay nightlife directory.

CINAEDVS game development. In 2026, CINAEDVS began developing several online games, with playable demos and prototypes published through its itch.io page.

Community work

Charity & fundraising

Fundraising and community work ran alongside Christina’s club and theatre career. She both performed and volunteered at charity events, including Shop Yourself Stupid, World AIDS Day fundraisers, Stonewall Hotel’s Red Party, ARQ’s Under the Mirrorball events and other LGBTQ and HIV/AIDS community fundraisers.

Shop Yourself StupidChristina performed and volunteered at Shop Yourself Stupid, including documented participation in 2009 and again in 2012.
