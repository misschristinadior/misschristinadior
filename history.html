History

A factual year-by-year record of Christina Dior’s career. Open a year to read it; the Performance Archive contains the deeper poster and repertoire collection.

2001Beginnings in Sydney

Beginnings in Sydney. Christina Dior began performing in Sydney's drag scene, developing a repertoire combining showgirl glamour, diva impersonations, dance-pop and theatrical comedy. Early appearances included Polly's Follies and the Venus Room. During her early ARQ Sydney years she also performed in Young Talent Mime, an early Thursday-evening show that gave emerging drag performers stage experience. The programme later evolved into Drag for Dollars, after Christina's involvement had ended. Christina also began developing the custom music-editing techniques that became a feature of her performances.

2004Connie and Carla promotion

Connie and Carla promotion. Christina participated in Sydney promotional activities for the film Connie and Carla, alongside Polly Petrie and Kirsten Damned. Surviving photographs document the performers travelling together in a convertible during the promotion.

2006Palace Hotel

Palace Hotel performances. Christina continued performing across Sydney's drag circuit, including a Kylie Minogue-themed performance at the Palace Hotel, preserved in photographs taken by Peteez/Imagez Photography.

2007Fashion design & music video

Fashion design and music video appearances. Christina Dior designed and constructed a floor-length pink gown from Supré shopping bags for the closing look of Supré's New Zealand Fashion Week presentation in Auckland. She also appeared alongside Kirsten Damned in the music video for Viva Las Vegas by Australian Elvis tribute performer She Is the King (Jacqueline Feilich).

2008Carlotta's Priscilla Show & DragTube

Carlotta's Priscilla Show. Christina Dior won the grand final of Carlotta's Priscilla Show competition at Stonewall Hotel, Sydney, receiving a $1,000 cash prize and a position in Carlotta's Priscilla Show. Although the original prize included a one-year position, Christina continued performing in the production until it stopped running. She also designed and constructed costumes for the show.

DragTube and original video production. Alongside nightclub performances, Christina developed and presented DragTube, an original video series documenting Sydney and regional LGBTQ nightlife through venue reports, street interviews, performances, backstage footage, travel and conversations with fellow entertainers. Episodes covered Castro's Nightclub in Wollongong; Sydney nightlife including Star City Casino and Taxi Club; Minnie Cooper's Beyoncé Experience; and regional performance travel including Canberra and Queensland. A Canberra performance documented in the series introduced Christina to the audience in connection with the Priscilla show.

Drag Queens of 2000–2010. Christina also produced a video retrospective preserving interviews, performances and recollections from Sydney's drag community, filmed at ARQ Sydney and the Lizard Lounge and featuring performers including Minnie Cooper and Kitty Glitter. The title describes the period documented rather than establishing the production's release year.

2009Recurring Sydney productions

Recurring Sydney productions. Christina became a regular presence at the Midnight Shift through Pretty Women, a recurring Friday-night drag show documented in contemporary Sydney press listings. Her work at Taxi Club included themed productions, comedy and musical numbers, and collaborations with other Sydney entertainers.

ReferencesStar Observer — Scene GuideStar Observer — What's On
2010DIVA Rising Star

DIVA Rising Star. Christina received the DIVA Rising Star Award, recognising her work within Sydney's drag entertainment scene. She continued hosting and performing at Stonewall Hotel, ARQ, Taxi Club, Midnight Shift and Sly Fox, including substitute appearances for other established performers. Notable engagements included a Madonna tribute at Sydney's final Sleaze Ball, The Reunion at the Burdekin Hotel, ARQ Christmas performances and Stonewall's New Year's Eve Masquerade Ball. Her work also included fundraising performances for community organisations.

Television — Underbelly: The Golden Mile. Christina also appeared in the third instalment of the Australian crime drama Underbelly, which depicted Sydney's Kings Cross nightclub and entertainment scene. Production began in Sydney in August 2009, and the series was broadcast on the Nine Network from 11 April to 27 June 2010.

2011Hosting, TV & national appearances

Hosting, television and national appearances. Christina appeared in numerous Sydney productions and community events, including Prisoner: Cell Block Heaven, Mardi Gras celebrations, ARQ's White Party marriage-equality fundraiser and Heaven Social Dance's anniversary fundraiser. She hosted Stonewall's Mardi Gras parade celebrations and continued regular hosting and performance work at Stonewall, ARQ and Taxi Club. Further credits included an Australian television commercial promoting Dolly Parton's Better Day tour, a Dolly Parton-themed Stonewall production and an appearance at Darwin Pride's Queens of the Galaxy. Her charitable work included performances benefiting Queensland flood victims and LGBTQ community organisations.

Television — Crownies. Christina's television work also included the ABC legal drama Crownies. The relevant storyline appears in Season 1, Episode 12, involving a legal dispute between two drag performers. The series began filming in Sydney in January 2011, and the episode was originally broadcast on 22 September 2011.

2012DIVA Miss Congeniality & press

DIVA Miss Congeniality and major press coverage. Christina and Minnie Cooper jointly received the DIVA Miss Congeniality Award. Christina was featured on the cover of City Hub in an article about the Taxi Club's reprieve from closure and performed in its Easter celebrations alongside Ophelia Shaft. Her other work included Monday Night Madness at Stonewall Hotel, where she appeared as a Cher impersonator, hosting Drag Bingo at Crows Nest Hotel, and performing at Parramatta's Rainbow Party. She also contributed guest appearances and community updates to Queer Out West radio.

The Delta Riggs music video. Christina appeared in the official music video for Rah Rah Radio by Australian rock band The Delta Riggs, credited as Miss Christina Dior. The appearance added to her earlier television-style productions and promotional video work.

2014International media coverage

International media coverage. A filmed performance of Whitney Houston's Saving All My Love for You at Sydney's Stonewall Hotel was selected for inclusion in Philadelphia Magazine's G Philly feature celebrating seven Whitney Houston drag performances. The American publication featured Christina alongside other drag artists, bringing her Australian performance footage to an international online audience.

Television production — Carlotta. Christina also worked on the Australian biographical telemovie Carlotta, about the legendary Kings Cross performer and Les Girls headliner. Production began in Sydney on 6 May 2013 and the film was first broadcast on ABC1 on 19 June 2014. This television credit was separate from Christina's earlier live work in Carlotta's Priscilla Show.

2015DIVA anniversary media

DIVA anniversary media appearance. Christina participated in the 25th anniversary DIVA Awards SX magazine cover shoot at Stonewall Hotel, alongside past award winners and other established Sydney performers.

2016Polly's Follies retrospective

Recognition in Sydney drag history. A historical retrospective celebrating 25 years of Polly's Follies identified Christina Dior among the performers who began their careers through the production, documenting her place in Sydney's earlier drag entertainment scene.

2017Prada's Priscillas touring

Prada's Priscillas touring production. Christina joined Prada Clutch's touring theatrical drag revue as one of the supporting showgirls alongside Conchita Grande, with performances extending beyond Sydney to regional Australian theatres.

ReferencesNewcastle Herald — A fab clutch of queens
2018Prada's Priscillas review

Prada's Priscillas touring production. Christina performed as one of Prada Clutch's supporting showgirls alongside Conchita Grande in Prada's Priscillas, a touring theatrical drag revue presented in regional Australian theatres. Appearances included Newcastle's Civic Theatre and Canberra Theatre. The Canberra production received a published review from the Canberra Critics Circle, which identified Christina by name and discussed the production's professionalism and elaborate staging.

ReferencesCanberra Critics Circle — Prada's Priscillas
2019Relocation to Europe

Relocation to Europe. Christina relocated from Australia to France, following nearly two decades of involvement in Sydney drag entertainment, hosting, theatre, video production and costume design. Following the move, her public performance work would soon pause during the COVID-19 pandemic.

2020–2021COVID-19 hiatus

COVID-19 hiatus. Christina's public performance activity paused during the COVID-19 pandemic, creating a break between the Australian/France era and the later return to performance and creative work in Berlin.

2022Return in Berlin & CINAEDVS

A new chapter in Berlin. After a break during the COVID-19 pandemic, Christina relocated to Berlin, Germany, and returned to creative activity within the city's queer nightlife and drag performance community. During this new period, Christina's creative brand Shimtastic was rebranded as CINAEDVS.

2023SchwuZ Drag Open Stage

SchwuZ Drag Open Stage. Christina joined SchwuZ's Drag Open Stage Class and appeared in the club's performance programme. Her appearances included the Pepsi Boston Bar Drag Open Stage and SchwuZ's 46th anniversary celebrations. German LGBTQ publication men named Christina among the performers appearing at the anniversary event.

2024Berlin & original music

Berlin performances and original music. Christina continued performing at SchwuZ, including the Pepsi Boston Bar's Lovegame and themed Drag Open Stage productions. She also produced promotional artwork for the venue. During the year, Christina began releasing self-written original music under the stylised recording name Christina Dior, with tracks produced and compiled in digital audio workstations (DAWs) for their final arrangements and mixes. Releases included Steal the Night, Time for Me, Tonight Tonight Tonight, Slay All Night and Midnight Glow, alongside multiple remixes and alternative arrangements.

ReferencesVictory Column — Love GameChristina Dïor on Apple Music
2025Original-music catalogue

Expansion of the original-music catalogue. Christina continued writing, producing and compiling original recordings in DAWs, releasing One Day Lover, Rail Me on the U-Bahn, BBC, May Day May Day and In the Darkroom. Her work incorporated dance music, electronic arrangements, vocal production, humour and themes drawn from nightlife and performance culture. Christina also continued developing accompanying artwork, remixes and video projects.

ReferencesChristina Dïor — Apple Music
2026Digital production, archive & Backroom

Digital stage production, archive preservation and nightlife media. Christina expanded her online creative work with a collection of original animated stage backgrounds designed for drag shows, concerts, karaoke and other performances. The videos attracted tens of thousands of views on YouTube. Alongside continued music and video development, she began assembling a comprehensive digital archive documenting 25 years of performances, productions, costumes, media appearances and original recordings. She also became the leading creative producer behind Backroom, a gay nightlife directory.
