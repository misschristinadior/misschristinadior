History

A factual chronology of Christina Dior’s performance career, grouped by year. Exact dates are kept in the underlying archive and used here only when a specific date is historically important.

Archive method. This page combines surviving posters, press and listings with Christina’s first-hand record of her own career. The more granular show-by-show material lives in the Performance Archive.
2001
  • BeginningsChristina Dior began performing in Sydney, developing an early style rooted in showgirl glamour, pop performance and queer nightlife.
  • Polly’s Follies / Venus RoomEarly appearances included the Polly’s Follies orbit and the Venus Room in Kings Cross, where Christina also developed music-editing and drag-DJ skills.

  • ChristinaDior.comAn early Christina Dior website was established during the first years of the career, creating a digital archive long before social media became the default home for performers.
PLACEHOLDER IMAGERepresentative photograph / poster for 2003
2004
  • Connie and Carla promotionChristina joined Polly Petrie and Kirsten Damned for a Sydney promotional appearance connected with the Australian release of the drag-themed film Connie and Carla.
PLACEHOLDER IMAGERepresentative photograph / poster for 2004
2006
  • Palace HotelA surviving 2006 performance photograph documents a Kylie-themed show at the Palace Hotel.
2008
  • Sydney circuitChristina continued performing across Sydney’s drag circuit, with appearances at ARQ, Taxi Club and other venues, as well as performance work with Polly Petrie and Kirsten Damned.
  • Fair DayShimtastic appeared on the Fair Day main stage, one of several Mardi Gras-season community appearances from this period.
2009
  • Taxi ClubThe Taxi Club became a major home for Christina’s themed shows and recurring productions, including Shimtastic events and weekly drag programming.
  • Midnight ShiftPretty Women became a recurring late-night Christina Dior show at the Midnight Shift.
2010
  • DIVA Rising StarChristina received the DIVA Rising Star award.
  • Sydney residenciesRegular performance work continued across Taxi Club, Stonewall Hotel, Midnight Shift, ARQ and Sly Fox, alongside frequent fill-in appearances for Maxi Shield, Verushka Darling and Tora Hymen.
  • Final Sleaze BallChristina appeared in the final Sleaze Ball, including a Madonna tribute.
  • The Reunion / XXXmasThe year also included The Reunion with Natasha Knowles and Penny Clifford and an XXXmas Eve appearance at ARQ with Farren Heit and Ophelia Shaft.
2011
  • Queensland Floods Drag-A-ThonChristina took part in the Queensland Floods Drag-A-Thon fundraiser. An earlier surviving reference recorded $3,150 raised.
  • Fair Day & Mardi GrasCommunity appearances included Fair Day and Mardi Gras-season hosting and performance work at Stonewall.
  • Prisoner: Cell Block HeavenChristina appeared in Prisoner: Cell Block Heaven at Heaven Social Dance, with further fundraising and community-event work during the year.
  • White PartyChristina appeared at ARQ’s White Party during the Sydney Pride Festival period.
  • Dolly Parton television commercialChristina appeared in an Australian television commercial promoting Dolly Parton’s Better Day tour alongside Maxi Shield and Laydee Kinme.
  • Queens of the GalaxyChristina travelled to Darwin for the inaugural Queens of the Galaxy event during Darwin Pride.
  • Stonewall & Taxi ClubThe year included extensive work at Stonewall, Taxi Club and ARQ, including themed nights, host slots and recurring productions.
2012
  • DIVA Miss CongenialityChristina and Minnie Cooper were joint recipients of the DIVA Miss Congeniality award.
  • Taxi Club reprieveCity Hub published “Hail the Taxi – Christina Dior celebrates the Taxi Club’s reprieve,” documenting Christina’s connection to the venue during its final years.
  • Rainbow Party & radioChristina appeared at the Rainbow Party in Parramatta and continued guest appearances and city update call-ins on Queer Out West 89.3 FM.
