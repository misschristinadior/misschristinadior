About Christina

Showgirl glamour with a pop heartbeat.

Hailing from Australia, Christina moved to Europe in 2019 and brought her showgirl spirit to Berlin in 2022. Inspired by her drag mothers Tora Hymen and Farren Heit and by performers including Wyness Mongrel-Bitch and Vanity Faire, Christina’s work blends new-millennium drag with classic showgirl glamour.

Her performances centre on dance and pop, theatrical edits, comedy and celebration — increasingly including original songs written and produced under the Christina Dïor name.
